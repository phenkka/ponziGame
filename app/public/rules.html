RULES

How to start ▼
  • Connect your Solana wallet
  • Buy $TOKENS and then you can buy packs
  • Each card has its own rarity, and for every card you get a certain number of tickets. By purchasing a card, you earn the right to participate in the daily jackpot.
How many tickets does each card give? ▼
  • Common: 10 tickets
  • Rare: 25 tickets
  • Epic: 60 tickets
  • Legendary: 100 tickets
The distribution rule for coins from purchased packs ▼
  • Burning: 25% of tokens go to token burning. This reduces the total supply of tokens, making each remaining token more valuable.
  • Referral rewards: 5% for regular users, 10% for inflows. When someone uses your referral code, you earn a percentage of their pack purchases.
  • Jackpot: 10% of tokens go to the daily jackpot, which is drawn every 24 hours. Only cards from purchased packs count towards jackpot tickets. Bonus packs (from predictions, daily check-ins) do not contribute to the jackpot.
  • Super Jackpot: 5% of tokens go to the super jackpot. It is awarded to the first user who collects the entire card collection. Once claimed, it cannot be claimed again, and the pool resets and starts accumulating for the next potential winner.
  • Developer fees: 5% of tokens go to the developers to support the platform's maintenance and development.
  • Pack-opening distribution: 40% of tokens are redistributed back through pack openings among users. This creates a circular economy where users can win tokens back by opening packs.
Daily Jackpot Rules ▼
  • The daily jackpot runs every 24 hours and resets automatically.
  • Your chance of winning is proportional to the number of tickets you have from purchased packs.
  • Only cards obtained from purchased packs count towards your tickets. Bonus packs do not contribute.
  • The winner receives 100% of the accumulated jackpot amount.
  • If no one has tickets, the jackpot rolls over to the next round.
  • Winners are selected randomly based on ticket distribution at the time of the draw.
Super Jackpot Rules ▼
  • To win the Super Jackpot, you must collect one of each card in the entire collection.
  • The Super Jackpot accumulates from 5% of all pack purchases.
  • Only the first user to complete the collection wins the current Super Jackpot.
  • After a Super Jackpot is claimed, the pool resets and starts accumulating again.
  • You can only win the Super Jackpot once per reset cycle.
  • The Super Jackpot is automatically awarded when you open a pack that completes your collection.
Battle Rules ▼
  • Battles allow you to compete with other players using your card collection.
  • Each battle requires specific cards or card combinations to participate.
  • Winners receive rewards based on the battle's prize pool.
  • Battles have entry requirements and time limits.
  • Your battle history and statistics are tracked in your profile.
Prediction Rules ▼
  • Make predictions on real-world events from Polymarket.
  • Each prediction has two possible outcomes (Yes/No, A/B).
  • You can place one bet per prediction.
  • If your prediction wins, you receive rewards based on probability distribution:
    • 40% chance: 3 Broken packs
    • 25% chance: 1 Common pack
    • 10% chance: 1 Legendary pack
    • 20% chance: 1 Common or Rare card
    • 5% chance: Personal legendary chance boost (+10% for 24 hours)
  • Cards from prediction rewards do not count towards jackpot tickets.
  • Predictions are resolved when the event outcome is determined.
Referral System Rules ▼
  • Share your unique referral code to invite new players.
  • You earn 5% of tokens from pack purchases made by users you referred (regular referrals).
  • You earn 10% of tokens from pack purchases made by users who joined through your referral link (inflow referrals).
  • Referral rewards are automatically credited to your account.
  • There is no limit on the number of users you can refer.
  • Your referral code can be found in your profile section.
Card Rarity and Pack Opening ▼
  • Each pack contains one random card based on probability distribution.
  • There is a chance to get nothing (loss) when opening a pack.
  • Card rarities from most common to rarest: Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
  • Higher rarity cards give more tickets but are rarer to obtain.
  • You can view your collection and card statistics in the "My Collection" section.
  • Duplicate cards increase your ticket count for that specific card.
