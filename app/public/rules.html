RULES

How to start
  • Connect your Solana wallet
  • Buy $TOKENS and then you can buy packs
  • Each card has its own rarity, and for every card you get a certain number of tickets. By purchasing a card, you earn the right to participate in the daily jackpot.
How many tickets does each card give?
  • Common: 10 tickets
  • Rare: 25 tickets
  • Epic: 60 tickets
  • Legendary: 100 tickets
The distribution rule for coins from purchased packs
  • Burning: 25% goes to token burning
  • Referral rewards: 5% for regular users, 10% for inflows
  • Jackpot: 10% of tokens go to the daily jackpot, drawn every 24 hours
  • Super Jackpot: 5% goes to the super jackpot and is awarded to the first user who collects the entire card collection; after it is claimed once, it cannot be claimed again and the pool starts accumulating again
  • Developer fees: 5% goes to the developers
  • Pack-opening distribution: 40% is redistributed back through pack openings among users
